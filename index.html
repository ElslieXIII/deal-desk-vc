What it was built for

One place for the whole investment cycle

A venture team spends its week between founders, co-investors, LPs and the companies it already owns. The information lives in spreadsheets, inboxes, board packs and people's heads, so it is easy to miss a follow-up, lose track of who has seen a deal, or walk into a meeting without the latest numbers.

Deal Desk VC brings sourcing, the pipeline, live deals, the portfolio and every relationship into one shared desk. It is built around the questions a fund asks every day: who should we talk to next, what needs a decision, which companies need help, and who might co-invest in or buy what we own.

What it does

  • Takes a company from first call through due diligence and investment committee to investment, tracking diligence workstreams, conviction, the round and the syndicate.
  • Monitors the portfolio: health ratings, cost against value, runway, a quarterly reporting tracker, fund-level returns and exit plans with buyer maps.
  • Scores sourced companies against investment theses and explains each score, with an AI second opinion and a pre-screen memo.
  • Keeps the CRM of founders, co-investors, LPs and acquirers, with plain-English search, such as "co-investors for a US$4m Series A in Nigeria".
  • Scans email for portfolio updates, new contacts and deal changes, and proposes them for approval.
  • Logs every change with who made it, and supports undo and redo.
How to use it

Getting around the desk

Summary
What needs attention today, across catch-ups, the pipeline, live deals, the portfolio and news.
Catch-up
Your to-do list: follow-ups, reminders, deal next steps, overdue reports and board dates.
Relationship management
Reminders to stay in touch, and a finder for people nobody has spoken to in a while.
Sourcing
Investment theses, the companies the sourcing agent found, and how well each one fits.
Pipeline
New companies from first call to screening. Move one into due diligence, park it, or pass with a reason.
Live deals
Deals in due diligence, IC, term sheet and closing, with the round, our cheque, valuation and syndicate.
Portfolio
Each company's health, value, runway, reporting and board dates, plus fund returns and exit plans.
CRM
Founders, co-investors, LPs, corporates and advisers, with filters and Ask Claude for plain-English search.
Recent conversations
Every meeting note and logged email, newest first.
News
Funding rounds, exits and regulation, matched to the portfolio and the pipeline.

Try this first

  1. Open Portfolio, filter Health → Red, then open the company.
  2. In Catch-up, open a reminder and click Meeting prep.
  3. In CRM, click Ask Claude and ask for co-investors for a deal.
  4. Click Email scan (top right), approve a suggestion, then press Undo.
  • Open anythingClick any company, person or deal name to see its full record, notes and history.
  • See it as someone elseChange Viewing as (top right) to see the desk from another team member's point of view.

How it's built

One desk, shared by the whole team

Shared and accountable

The team works from the same records. Every change is logged with who made it and can be undone.

AI where it saves time

Meeting briefings from notes, emails, deals and news; plain-English search for co-investors and buyers; second opinions and pre-screen memos on sourced companies; CRM updates suggested after each note.

Plain web technology

A single-page app in plain JavaScript and CSS. It runs inside Claude, with a database the whole team shares.
